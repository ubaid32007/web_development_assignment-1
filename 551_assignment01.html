About the Portal

The UET Mardan Student Activities Portal is a platform created to bring students together beyond the classroom. It highlights technical, sports and cultural events organized by university societies, making it easy for every student to stay informed and take part.

Our goal is to encourage creativity, teamwork and leadership by giving students the opportunity to showcase their talents and build lasting connections on campus.
